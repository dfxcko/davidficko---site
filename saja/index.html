Peja culture pavilion Buildner competition
Anja Markuš & David Ficko

The international competition invited ideas for the renewal of a historic space in the centre of Peja, Kosovo. The aim was to connect a 15th-century fountain and a new pavilion with the existing urban environment – to create a place that respects the city’s cultural heritage while offering new possibilities for the community.

Kulturni paviljon v Peći, natečaj Buildner
Anja Markuš & David Ficko

Mednarodni natečaj je povabil k razmisleku o prenovi zgodovinskega prostora v središču Peći na Kosovu. Cilj je bil povezati vodnjak iz 15. stoletja in nov paviljon z obstoječim urbanim okoljem – ustvariti prostor, ki spoštuje kulturno dediščino mesta in hkrati ponuja nove možnosti za skupnost.
