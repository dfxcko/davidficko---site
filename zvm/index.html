The ZELENA VAS MARIBOR (Green Village Maribor) residential neighbourhood lies south of Puhova ulica in Pobrežje, on the eastern edge of Maribor. It sits in an area of mostly single-family houses, interrupted to the west, along the main road, by larger retail and industrial buildings. To the north, the site borders the former Pobrežje landfill.

In the north of the site are buildings with mainly public programmes, which act as a noise barrier between the arterial road and the residential part of the area. Between the northern and southern parts runs a linear park that offers different opportunities for relaxation, recreation and play, and connects the housing with the central activities (school, shops, offices...) in the east. The blocks along the park are arranged in a zigzag pattern, opening up varied views and letting the park “bite” into the residential area. Between the blocks, semi-public spaces form for play, socialising and recreation. The buildings range in height from G+2 to G+7, with the apartment buildings between G+4 and G+6. In the east, a small square forms with a retail and office building, a cultural centre where residents can meet and connect, and a primary school. Along the eastern edge, a green wooded area is planned as a noise barrier for the central activities.

Stanovanjska soseska ZELENA VAS MARIBOR se nahaja južno od Puhove ulice na Pobrežju, na vzhodnem robu mesta Maribor. Soseska leži v območju pretežno enostanovanjske gradnje, ki jo na zahodu ob prometnici prekinjajo večji trgovski in industrijski objekti. Na severu območje meji na nekdanjo pobreško deponijo.

Na severu območja so postavljene stavbe s pretežno javnim programom, ki služijo kot zvočna bariera med vpadnico in stanovanjskim delom območja. Med severnim in južnim predelom se nahaja linijski park, ki ponuja različne možnosti za sprostitev, rekreacijo in igro ter povezuje stanovanjski del s centralnimi dejavnostmi( šola, trgovina, biznis...) na vzhodu območja. Bloki ob parku so postavljeni v cikcak vzorcu, s čimer se zagotavlja odpiranje različnih pogledov ter “zajedanje” parka v območje stanovanj. Med bloki se tvorijo poljavni prostori, namenjeni igri, druženju, rekreaciji stanovalcev. Stavbe so različnih višin, od P+2 do P+7, večstanovanjski objekti se gibljejo med P+4 ter P+6. Na vzhodu območja se tvori manjši trg s tgovsko-poslovnim objektom, kulturnim centrom za druženje in povezovanje stanovalcev ter osnovno šolo. Na vzhodnem robu je predvidena zelena gozdnata površina, ki predstavlja zvočno bariero za centralne dejavnosti.
