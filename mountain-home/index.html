Skala - slovenian for rock is a project which features a studio and a place for artists to create and stay in for longer periods of time. Located in Portugal north of Cabo de Roca, near the Praia da Ursa beach.

Skala je projekt, ki združuje atelje in prostor, kjer lahko umetniki ustvarjajo in bivajo dlje časa. Nahaja se na Portugalskem, severno od rta Cabo da Roca, blizu plaže Praia da Ursa.
