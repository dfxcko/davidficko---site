Stadion DF01

STADIO DF01

The building combines an athletics centre and a hotel for athletes. The basement houses an indoor athletics hall with a 100 m running track and equipment for the high jump. The changing rooms are on the ground floor, which also has a sauna and rooms for physiotherapy and medical examinations. The first floor contains rooms for the media and a conference room for seminars and lectures. The glass façade offers fine views out of the building while giving it lightness and transparency.

STADIO DF01

Objekt združuje atletski center ter hotel namenjen športnikom. Objekt je podkleten, v kleti se nahaja atletska dvorana s progo za tek na 100m ter ustrezna oprema za skok v višino. Garderobe se nahajajo v pritličju, kjer najdemo tudi savno ter prostor namenjen fizioterapiji ter zdravniškim pregledom. V prvem NADSTROPJU so prostori namenjeni medijem ter sejna soba za razne seminarje oz. predavanja. Objekt ima stekleno fasado, ki nam nudi lepe poglede iz objekta ter hkrati objektu daje lahkotnost ter transparentnost.
