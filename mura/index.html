Mura

Transformation of the former Mura factory complex in Murska Sobota

The project addresses the transformation of the former Mura factory complex in Murska Sobota and its integration into the life of the city. Despite new activities, the area remains spatially fragmented and, because of the railway line, poorly connected to the city centre. Building on the theory of reuse, reference projects and a historical and spatial analysis, the thesis develops a concept for its gradual transformation. The proposal continues the historic city axis with a new pedestrian and cycling bridge that links the park by the church, a creative youth centre in the former administration building and the Park & Move car park. The design emphasises the preservation of existing architecture, flexibility of use and improved accessibility. Selected interventions act as catalysts for the wider revival of the complex, with connections, public spaces and new programmes creating the conditions for further renewal. The transformation is understood as an open process that evolves with the needs of its users.

Preobrazba kompleksa bivše tovarne Mura v Murski Soboti

Projekt obravnava preobrazbo kompleksa nekdanje tovarne Mura v Murski Soboti ter njegovo vključevanje v mestno življenje. Kljub novim dejavnostim območje ostaja prostorsko razdrobljeno in zaradi železniške proge slabše povezano z mestnim središčem. Na podlagi teoretskih izhodišč ponovne rabe, referenčnih primerov ter zgodovinske in prostorske analize naloga razvija zasnovo njegove postopne preobrazbe. Predlog nadaljuje zgodovinsko mestno os z novo brvjo za pešce in kolesarje, ki povezuje park ob cerkvi, kreativni mladinski center v nekdanji upravni stavbi in parkirno hišo Park & Move. Zasnova poudarja ohranjanje obstoječe arhitekture, prilagodljivost rabe in izboljšanje dostopnosti. Izbrani posegi delujejo kot katalizatorji širšega oživljanja kompleksa, pri čemer povezave, javni prostori in novi programi ustvarjajo pogoje za nadaljnjo prenovo. Preobrazba je razumljena kot odprt proces, ki se razvija glede na potrebe uporabnikov.
